My story is about beating the odds. In my home, even whispering about dating was treated like a major felony – totally and hilariously forbidden. But that's exactly what got me interested in figuring out how people connect. I've always had this knack for making sense of relationships, even when they were a bit of a mystery at home.

Transitioning from Biology and Business Administration to Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, life coaching, and now delving into Neuroscience and Psychology is an impressive journey. This mix of disciplines offered me a unique perspective on both the scientific and emotional aspects of human behaviour.

But here's the interesting bit – my lifelong romance with the concept of love! From my early days in junior school, I was the unofficial 'love guru,' stationed at my desk for all my lovesick friends. That's when my wild journey began, driven by my burning desire to figure out how humans connect and feel all those emotions stuff. My expertise? Well, it started with my young curiosity about love and relationships and ended up with me taking on the dating world like a pro. I mix a bit of old-school charm with a whole lot of modern excitement. The dating world in 2024 needs a revolution. My goal? To help people build real, face-to-face connections built on things like honesty, trust, respect, and a sprinkle of courage and playful flirting.

Whether you're a hopeless romantic or a sceptic in love, join me in resetting the dating scene, one heart at a time.
